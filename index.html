Process Overview — Solvent-Assisted Fatty-Acid Alkyd Route

This simulation illustrates the synthesis of an alkyd polyester resin from tall oil fatty acids, polyol (glycerol/pentaerythritol), and phthalic anhydride solids in the presence of xylene azeotropic solvent.

Reaction & Azeotropic Overhead

Water formed during esterification condenses in horizontal condenser E-201 and decants in separator V-201. Solvent returns by gravity to R-101; reaction water accumulates in closed receiver TK-201.

Downstream Thinning & Packaging

Following simulated endpoint release, resin is transferred through P-201 to blender BL-301, thinned with solvent, cooled via cooling water, polished through filter F-301, and staged in tank TK-401 for container filling FL-401.
