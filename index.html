ABOUT PLANTSCOUT

Explore before entry.

PlantScout is a software-only inspection simulation of an epoxy-resin solution unit. The upper process level shows a batch reactor, reflux condenser, separator and blend/holding vessel; B1 is the lower pump and utility gallery with filtered-product transfer.

The process sequence is based on a public EIA process description; the 3D facility layout is an original simulation, not a replica of that factory. All hazard readings are synthetic. No real plant, calibrated sensor or safety system is connected, and the report cannot authorize human entry.
